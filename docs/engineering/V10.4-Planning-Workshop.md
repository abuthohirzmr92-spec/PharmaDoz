# 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Sprint V10.4 adalah sprint keempat dan TERAKHIR dari 4-sprint evolutionary refactor yang dimulai dari V10.1 Blueprint. Sprint ini mengorkestrasi seluruh domain service yang telah dibangun (AllocationBuilder, PricingEngine, AllocationValidator) ke dalam `CheckoutSessionService` dan membekukan transaksi melalui `TransactionFreezer`.

# 8. RISK REGISTER

## Architecture Risks

| ID | Risk | Prob | Impact | Severity | Mitigation |
|----|------|:----:|:------:|:--------:|------------|
| A1 | CheckoutSession state machine over-engineered | Low | Medium | LOW | Start with 2 states (DRAFT, FROZEN). Add intermediate states incrementally |
| A2 | TransactionFreezer changes `finalizeTransaction()` behavior | Medium | Critical | **HIGH** | Dual-write: old path runs parallel. Comparison validation |
| A3 | Repository adapter breaks existing DB queries | Low | High | MEDIUM | Adapters wrap existing repos — no query changes |

## Engineering Risks

| ID | Risk | Prob | Impact | Severity | Mitigation |
|----|------|:----:|:------:|:--------:|------------|
| E1 | CheckoutSessionService coupling with store logic | Medium | High | **HIGH** | Service wraps store initially. Gradual delegation |
| E2 | Wiring changes break checkout total | Low | Critical | **HIGH** | Pricing comparison: 30+ scenarios before merge |
| E3 | Legacy field removal breaks consumers | Medium | Medium | MEDIUM | `@deprecated` since V10.2 — consumers already migrated |

## Business Risks

| ID | Risk | Prob | Impact | Severity | Mitigation |
|----|------|:----:|:------:|:--------:|------------|
| B1 | Checkout breaks in production | Low | Critical | **HIGH** | Rollback: 3 hours. Zero data impact. Dual-write safety net |
