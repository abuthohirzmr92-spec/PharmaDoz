# EEOS — Partner Architecture v1.0

## Partner Types

| Type | Role | Requirements | Benefits |
|------|------|-------------|----------|
| **Architecture Partner** | Build certified engines, contribute to core | 2+ certified engines, Architecture Board participation | Early access, co-marketing, revenue share |
| **Technology Partner** | Integrate EEOS with their platform | Proven integration, compatibility testing | Partner badge, joint marketing |
| **Consulting Partner** | Provide EEOS implementation services | Certified consultants on staff, reference clients | Partner directory, lead referrals |
| **Training Partner** | Deliver EEOS training and certification | Approved curriculum, certified instructors | Official training badge, course marketplace |
| **Certification Partner** | Review and certify community engines | Architecture Board membership, review track record | Certification authority, trust badge |
| **Community Maintainer** | Maintain community engines and plugins | Active contributions, code review participation | Commit access, community recognition |

## Governance

- Partners apply via Architecture Board
- Annual review for continued partnership
- Violations: warning → suspension → termination
- Revenue share (where applicable): defined in partner agreement
